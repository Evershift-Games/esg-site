About Evershift

Independent by design. Ambitious in scope.

We create original entertainment experiences for mobile players. Our goal is to combine accessible play with meaningful decisions, strong progression, and systems that remain rewarding over time.

Evershift Games develops its own intellectual property from its Singapore base with a globally distributed team. Our first title is being built for mobile platforms, including iPhone and iPad, and is intended for players around the world.

What we do

A full game-development studio.

Our work spans the creative, technical, and operational disciplines required to build and support a modern mobile game.

01

Game design & systems

Core gameplay, progression, economy, balance, and the systems that create clear goals and lasting engagement.

02

Engineering & online services

Client architecture, tools, backend integration, data, and the technology needed to operate a reliable mobile title.

03

World & content production

Original settings, characters, visual direction, and content designed as a coherent player experience.

04

Live game development

Iteration, player feedback, analytics, events, and ongoing improvements after the game reaches its audience.

Our first project

An original mid-core mobile game is in development.

The project is being created for an international audience, with particular attention to mobile players across Asia. It is currently in development for iOS and other mobile platforms.

We are not yet sharing the title, artwork, or release date publicly. More information will be announced when the project is ready.

The studio

Built across borders, led from Singapore.

Evershift brings together game-development experience across architecture, engineering, design, production, and creative work.

We work as a distributed team across locations and time zones, using a shared production process and a common technical foundation. This structure allows us to assemble specialist talent while maintaining one clear product vision.
